Meet the doctors.

Two doctors, one standard — you'll see the same faces at every visit, so your care stays personal from consultation to completion.

Dr. Natalia Bartkova, DDS

General, restorative & cosmetic dentistry · NYU College of Dentistry

For Dr. Bartkova, the appointment isn't finished until you understand it. With more than 30 years in practice — two decades of them in New York City — she's known for slowing down, walking patients through every option in plain language, and never rushing a decision. Her philosophy: a healthy smile starts with strong teeth and healthy gums, not just a white one. Anxious patients tend to leave her chair wondering what they were nervous about.

Dr. Yuriy Kaziyev, DDS

Implant, restorative & cosmetic dentistry · NYU College of Dentistry

Dr. Kaziyev has spent more than 25 years restoring smiles, most of them caring for families in Brooklyn before bringing his practice to Fort Lauderdale. He specializes in the work that changes how a smile looks and functions — implants, same-day crowns, full-arch restorations, and Invisalign — all planned digitally so results are precise and comfortable. Outside the office, he's an avid traveler who's always planning the next trip.
